How the priority score works

0–100, higher = renovate sooner

  • Condition: up to 50 points (1 Critical = 50, 5 Excellent = 0).
  • Model: Older V6 = 20, V6 = 10, V7 = 0.
  • Signboard: 5 points per problem (rough, skin change, lights dim), up to 15.
  • No back-lit logo at reception: 5 points.
  • Years since last renovation: up to 10 (8+ years = 10; unknown year = 5).
  • Bands: Urgent 61–100, High 46–60, Medium 31–45, Low 0–30.

Where the numbers come from

Definitions

  • Centres in scope = rows in the Centers tab with "In Survey" = Yes. Pickup points are excluded.
  • Survey answers come from the Repository tab. If a centre was submitted more than once, the latest submission is used.
  • Coverage = centres surveyed ÷ centres in scope.
  • Issue themes are keyword matches on the free-text comments, so one comment can count under several themes.
  • Data is read live from the Google Sheet (read-only) and refreshes every 5 minutes, or with Refresh data above the dashboard.
